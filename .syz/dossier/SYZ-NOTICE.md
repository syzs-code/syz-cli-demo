# SYZYGY — Risk, Privacy & Responsibility Notice

This document tells you what to watch for when using SYZYGY: where outputs are **non-deterministic**, where data **leaves your machine**, what **real systems** your tests touch, and where **responsibility** sits. It is informational — it does not change how any command behaves. Use of the `syz` CLI is governed by the `LICENSE` (EULA) at the repository root.

## AI is non-deterministic — human review is mandatory

SYZYGY has two AI surfaces: the `{{ ai: <instruction> }}` resolver (AI **generates** a value) and the `operator: ai` assertion (AI **judges** a value). Both call a large language model, and an LLM can return a different answer for the same input, be wrong with confidence, or drift as the provider updates the model.

- **Review AI-generated data** (`{{ ai: }}`) before trusting a run built on it — a fabricated id, amount, or payload that *looks* valid can mask a real defect or invent one.
- **Review AI-evaluated verdicts** (`operator: ai`) — an `ai` assertion that passes is the model's opinion, not a deterministic check. For anything that must be certain, prefer a deterministic operator (`equals`, `matches`, `json` path checks) and reserve `ai` for genuinely fuzzy criteria.
- AI features are **opt-in**: a suite that uses no `{{ ai: }}` resolver and no `operator: ai` assertion is fully deterministic.

## Data egress & privacy

When an AI feature runs, the values it needs are **sent to the third-party model provider** configured in `.syz/ai-api.yaml` (the `{{ ai: }}` instruction and any `{{ }}` it interpolates; the asserted value and `instruction` for `operator: ai`). That data leaves your machine.

- **Do not put secrets or PII into AI instructions or AI-asserted values.** Keep credentials in `{{ os-env-var:NAME }}` and out of anything an AI feature reads.
- You choose the provider and endpoint (any OpenAI-compatible API). **Data residency, retention, and GDPR/CCPA obligations are yours** — pick a provider and region that match your policy.
- Non-AI execution (HTTP sends, validates, the ledger, reports) stays local except for the actual requests your scenarios make to the systems under test.

## Provider terms & cost

The AI provider is a relationship between **you and them**. Their API terms, acceptable-use policy, rate limits, and **billing** apply to every AI call `syz` makes on your behalf. SYZYGY does not proxy, meter, or subsidise provider usage.

## Execution safety — tests hit real systems

`syz run` performs the **real** calls your scenarios and plugins describe — live HTTP requests today, and (Phase 2) real Kafka/RabbitMQ/SFTP/database/object-store operations. There is no built-in sandbox.

- Point environments at the **right** target. A scenario run against production *is* a production action.
- Negative and destructive tests do real work — review what a scenario does before running it against shared or live infrastructure.

## Anti-hallucination directive (for the authoring agent)

When authoring plugins or scenarios, **never invent a capability that SYZYGY does not have.** If a test needs an operator, flag, field, resolver prefix, executor, or syntax that does not exist in `SYZ-MANUAL.md`, **stop and say so** — state the specific missing capability and why the test cannot be authored as asked — rather than fabricating one that "looks right." A fabricated construct does not silently pass: `syz lint` rejects unknown schema/identifiers (`UNKNOWN_*`, and the reserved-`syz` / unknown-`ctx.var` checks), so an invented field fails loudly later. Self-reporting the gap up front is faster and honest; do not paper over a missing feature.
